# Language Coach: Fix My Message — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 12 November 2026*

Language Coach is an app for the Anna platform that helps you fix a message (an email, chat or LinkedIn message) before you send it, and learn from your mistakes. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## What the app sends

When you tap **Make it send-ready** (or **Try another tone**), the message you typed is sent to **Anna's built-in AI** (through Anna's `llm.complete` service) together with your choices (who it's for, where it's going, the tone), your language settings and a short summary of your most common past mistake types, so the AI can check for them.

- Language Coach has **no servers of its own**. It does not send your text to any other company, website or service.
- It uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- The AI processing is done by Anna and is covered by Anna's own terms and privacy policy. Each check uses your Anna AI allowance.

## What the app stores

Language Coach stores a small amount of data in **your own Anna app storage**, which is private to your Anna account and this app:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Settings (the language you write in, explanation language, app language) | So you don't have to set them up again |
| History: your last 10 checks (your message, the result, and who / where / tone) | So you can reopen recent messages |
| Saved messages: messages you tap ☆ Save on (up to 100) | Your personal list |
| Mistake patterns: for each type of mistake (for example "prepositions"), how often it happened and your latest example | To show "Your top mistakes" and check extra carefully for them |

Nothing else is stored. The app does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

## Sharing

The app only shares something when you tap **Send** or **Copy**. It then hands the text to your device's share sheet or clipboard, and you choose where it goes.

## Deleting your data

- **Saved messages** and **History**: open 🕘 → *Clear all*, or delete single items.
- **Everything**: open ⚙️ Settings → *Delete all my data*. This removes your settings, history, saved messages and mistake patterns (and any data from older versions of the app) from your Anna storage.

## Children

Language Coach is a general-audience app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and it tells the AI never to produce sexual or hateful content.
