# Language Coach: Fix My English — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 27 October 2026*

Language Coach is an app for the Anna platform that helps you practise English and other languages. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## What the app sends

When you use **Fix My Sentence**, **Say It Naturally** or the **Daily Challenge**, the text you type is sent to **Anna's built-in AI** (through Anna's `llm.complete` service) so it can create corrections, explanations, challenges and grades. Your language settings are sent with it.

- Language Coach has **no servers of its own**. It does not send your text to any other company, website or service.
- It uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- The AI processing is done by Anna and is covered by Anna's own terms and privacy policy. Each request uses your Anna AI allowance.

## What the app stores

Language Coach stores a small amount of data in **your own Anna app storage**, which is private to your Anna account and this app:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Settings (language you're learning, explanation language, style, app language) | So you don't have to set them up again |
| History: your last 10 Fix My Sentence checks (your text and the result) | So you can reopen recent checks |
| Saved phrases: phrases you tap ⭐ Save on (up to 100) | Your personal phrase list |
| Streak: a count and the date of your last completed challenge | To show your daily streak |
| Today's challenge: the task, your answer and its grade | So the challenge stays the same all day |

Nothing else is stored. The app does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera or microphone.

## Sharing

The app only shares something when you tap **Share** or **Copy**. It then hands the text to your device's share sheet or clipboard, and you choose where it goes.

## Deleting your data

- **Saved phrases** and **History**: open ⭐ → *Clear all*, or delete single items.
- **Everything**: open ⚙️ Settings → *Delete all my data*. This removes your settings, history, saved phrases, streak and today's challenge from your Anna storage.

## Children

Language Coach is a general-audience learning app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and it tells the AI never to produce sexual or hateful content.
